Insights from the full dataset

  1. Platform leadership. Netflix earns the most (₹17,641 Cr, 24.6%), then Amazon Prime Video (23.3%). Apple TV+ is smallest at 4.3%.
  2. Quality vs platform. Disney+ Hotstar has the best average IMDb rating (6.84), SonyLIV the lowest (6.60). Bigger revenue does not mean better-rated content.
  3. Genre revenue and ROI. Action earns the most (₹19,376 Cr), yet Horror returns the best ROI (179%) and Fantasy the lowest (28%).
  4. Language mix. Hindi is 38.9% of titles, then English (20.9%). Korean titles rate highest on average (7.08).
  5. Movies vs web series. 370 movies and 280 web series. Web series average 6.86 IMDb against 6.69 for movies.
  6. Release trend. 42.0% of titles came out in 2023–2025, and 2025 peaked with 109 titles.
  7. Audience maturity. U/A is the largest group (45.2%), and adult (A) titles make up 39.2%.

What is inside the workbook

Raw Data (675 records) → Cleaned Data (650 titles, with Profit and ROI % added) → Pivot Tables (platform, genre, year) → Charts → Dashboard (KPI cards and dropdown filters) → Insights with a data-cleaning log.

Cleaning fixed inconsistent text such as "HORROR " and "amazon prime video ", units like "174 min", duplicates, and missing ratings.
